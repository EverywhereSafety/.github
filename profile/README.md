# Everywhere Safety

**Safety and privacy for AI embedded in everyday life.**

As capable agents learn from feedback, act over long horizons, and enter changing physical and social environments, safety must move with them. We study how AI systems understand intent, context, state, and privacy—and build practical ways to make their actions safer.

Our work spans:

- **Red teaming:** exposing failures hidden across turns, tools, and changing state.
- **Blue teaming:** building defenses that reason over intent, context, and feedback.
- **Privacy:** protecting people as AI enters physical and social environments.
