# Everywhere Safety

**Safety and privacy for AI embedded in everyday life.**

As capable agents learn from feedback, act over long horizons, and enter changing physical and social environments, safety must move with them. We study how AI systems understand intent, context, state, and privacy—and build practical ways to make their actions safer.

Our work has two connected branches:

- **Attack → Defense:** expose failures and build defenses across knowledge, dialogue, tools, and changing state — [CKA-Agent](https://everywheresafety.github.io/cka-agent/) ([code](https://github.com/EverywhereSafety/CKA-Agent)), [TurnGate](https://everywheresafety.github.io/turngate/) ([code](https://github.com/EverywhereSafety/TurnGate)), and [SEAD](https://everywheresafety.github.io/sead/) ([code](https://github.com/EverywhereSafety/SEAD)).
- **Privacy:** protect people as AI enters physical and social environments — [EAPrivacy](https://everywheresafety.github.io/ea-privacy/) ([code](https://github.com/EverywhereSafety/EAPrivacy)) and [ImmersedPrivacy](https://everywheresafety.github.io/immerse-privacy/) ([code](https://github.com/EverywhereSafety/ImmersedPrivacy)).
